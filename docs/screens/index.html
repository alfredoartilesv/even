Components

Screens are built from the shared UI kit in src/ui/. Tests (tests/uiLayout.test.ts) keep screens from drawing their own titles, tab rows or icons.

ComponentFileUsed for
PageHeadersrc/ui/layout.tsxThe sticky title row of every tab: back button or profile picture, title and subtitle, actions (sync, History, New).
SectionHeadersrc/ui/layout.tsxSection headings with a link (Workouts › Your activity, Meal prep › Open).
QuickActionssrc/ui/layout.tsxLog food, Log a drink, Ask Claude, Check-in on the Dashboard.
ScaleRatingsrc/ui/controls.tsxMood and energy, 1 to 5 in words, on the check-in.
Switchsrc/ui/controls.tsxOn/off settings as rows: what Claude may see, the sync reminder.
MenuListsrc/ui/layout.tsxThe Settings menu.
SubTabssrc/ui/layout.tsxThe views inside a Meal prep group.
Segmentedsrc/ui/layout.tsxDay / Week / Month on the Dashboard; Food log / Meal prep.
Ringsrc/ui/charts/figures.tsxCalories eaten against the target and steps against the goal.
ChipGroup, RangePickersrc/ui/controls.tsxPeriods and filters: Alcohol periods, profile activity ranges, chart ranges.
Iconsrc/ui/icons.tsxTab bar icons, the chat's camera and send buttons.
TabBarsrc/app/TabBar.tsxThe five tabs at the bottom.
StatTile, Meter, BarChart, LineChart, CalendarHeatmapsrc/ui/charts/Numbers and charts: Dashboard tiles and charts, goals, food history, supermarket spending.
StatusBadge, Steppersrc/ui/controls.tsxStates as icon and words; portion counts.
CalorieMeter, MacroSplitsrc/ui/nutrition.tsxCalories against the target, macro shares (Log food).
StockSummary, DefrostTimeline, StockAlerts, BatchBarsrc/ui/mealprep/Meal prep stock and the defrost plan.
